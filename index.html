Open Turnitin My Files View upload guide
Before uploading: select the correct course and semester. Submit each assignment only once unless your lecturer asks you to resubmit.
  1. 1
    Choose your class.

    Use the Winter Semester 2026/2027 list for current courses. Use Previous Semesters only for older assignments.

  2. 2
    Open the submission page.

    Select Upload assignment beside the correct class. Sign in to OVGU Turnitin when requested.

  3. 3
    Check the assignment details.

    Confirm that the class and assignment name match your course before choosing a file.

  4. 4
    Name and select your file.

    Name the assignment file using the professor's name, semester, and academic year. Examples: ProfessorName_WinterSemester_2026-2027.pdf or ProfessorName_SummerSemester_2027.pdf. Then select the final version from your device.

  5. 5
    Complete the upload.

    Enter a submission title if requested, continue through the confirmation screen, and wait until Turnitin confirms the upload.

  6. 6
    Verify your submission.

    Open My Files and confirm that the correct document appears. Keep the digital receipt or confirmation for your records.
